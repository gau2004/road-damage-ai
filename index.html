How it works

  1. Images are resized, normalized, and (optionally) augmented.
  2. A MobileNetV2 backbone (transfer learning) extracts features.
  3. A small dense head classifies the image as Broken or Not Broken Road.
  4. Training produces real accuracy/loss graphs and a confusion matrix — nothing is hardcoded.
